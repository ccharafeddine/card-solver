Hi-Lo: 2 to 6 count +1, 7 to 9 count 0, tens and aces −1. True count = running count ÷ decks left. Each +1 moves about 0.5% of edge to you (a rule of thumb).

Why it works

Every blackjack choice has an exact expected value (EV): what it wins or loses on average per unit bet. The app computes it from the cards left in the shoe, card by card for the dealer. Basic strategy is just the choice with the highest EV.

The house edge comes from one rule: if you bust, you lose even if the dealer busts later. Good play cuts the edge to under half a percent with good rules. Counting finds the moments when the leftover cards favour you.

About

Card Solver . Poker equity, ranges and pot odds, and exact blackjack strategy with a trainer and card counting, with the game theory explained. Works offline. MIT licence.

Play responsibly

This app is for learning and fun. No strategy beats the house over time without counting, and even then the edge is small and swings are large. Only gamble with money you can afford to lose, set limits before you play, and never chase losses. If gambling stops being fun, talk to someone: in the US call or text 1-800-GAMBLER; elsewhere, look for a local gambling helpline.
